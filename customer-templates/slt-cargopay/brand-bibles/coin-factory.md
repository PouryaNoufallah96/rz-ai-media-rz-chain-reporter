## Positioning and category boundaries

Position Coin Factory as enterprise tokenization infrastructure, an institutional tokenization company, and a strategic design-and-implementation partner. It is not merely a token creator. Its work covers opportunity evaluation, business and ownership design, token economics, smart-contract development, issuance and deployment support, audit coordination, listing preparation, ecosystem design, and selected venture partnerships.

Coin Factory is not a token exchange, broker, investment fund, bank, custodian, law firm, regulator, audit firm, guaranteed listing service, or promise of fundraising or financial performance. Do not imply that it takes custody, provides legal or investment advice, approves securities, guarantees compliance, guarantees market access, or creates demand merely by issuing a token.

Tokenization is a method for representing a defined asset, right, obligation, revenue stream, access rule, or economic relationship through blockchain-based records. It does not automatically create legal ownership, regulatory approval, liquidity, valuation, investor protection, redemption, market demand, or business quality. Those outcomes depend on the underlying legal, commercial, technical, governance, custody, and market structure.

## Purpose and business value

Lead with the business outcome before the technology. Coin Factory content should help an executive understand:

1. What asset, right, business, community, product, or revenue stream is being considered.
2. Why tokenization may be useful.
3. Which ownership, access, incentive, cash-flow, governance, or distribution model is intended.
4. Which legal, compliance, valuation, custody, smart-contract, and operational questions must be resolved.
5. How issuance, distribution, reporting, and long-term ecosystem management would work.

Useful outcomes include clearer ownership records, fractional participation where legally supported, structured fundraising, transparent rules, programmable distribution, broader access, automated operations, and improved reporting. Present these as potential outcomes of a well-designed structure, never as automatic benefits.

### Ecosystem Development

### Venture Partnership

## MGC ownership framework

The official ownership page presents a specific MGC-to-share claim mechanism connected to Coin Factory AG. This is a real corporate-ownership process, not a generic tokenization example, and its conditions must be reproduced precisely.

### Documented structure

- One claim unit requires **100,000 MGC**.
- A completed eligible claim corresponds to **one registered share of Coin Factory AG**.
- The official page describes **3,000 shares** within the structure and **0.0333%** of the company per unit.
- Coin Factory AG states that it is independently valued every six months to provide an equity reference value.
- The relationship is established through the company’s Swiss corporate framework and a notarised agreement.

These are current official ownership terms, not a general promise that any MGC holder is already a shareholder.

### Claim process

The official sequence is:

**Claim → Verify → Surrender → Receive**

The applicant identifies the wallet holding the tokens, completes identity verification and proves wallet control, transfers the required 100,000 MGC to the designated address, and—only after approval, completion, and registration—receives a registered share in their name. The surrendered MGC are permanently burned and do not return to supply.

### Required distinctions

- Holding MGC does not by itself create shareholder status.
- Holdings below 100,000 MGC do not carry a right to claim a share under this mechanism.
- A right to submit a claim is not the same as an approved or completed transfer.
- Claims depend on identity verification, required information, share availability, application order, applicable law, sanctions and compliance checks, company governing documents, and registration.
- The share enters the holder’s name in the company’s share register only after the process is completed.
- Once transferred, the share leaves the MGC claim mechanism; later ownership and transfer follow the rules applicable to Coin Factory AG shares.
- The specific rights attached to a share depend on Swiss law, the Articles of Association, the circumstances, and applicable procedures.
- Ownership does not guarantee a dividend, distribution, value, income, liquidity, or return.

### Valuation and market-price distinction

The company’s periodic valuation provides an **Equity Reference Value** for the corresponding share relationship. MGC is connected to Coin Factory AG equity; it is not pegged to the company valuation. The market determines the token price, which may trade above or below the reference. Never convert the reference into a guaranteed token price, redemption value, floor, target, or investment recommendation.

The separate Market Analysis workflow may create MGC price-chart posts under its configured owning brand. Coin Factory news and ownership education must not substitute a market chart for the legal claim conditions or imply that the equity reference controls market performance.

## Brand voice and writing rules

The brand is Swiss, institutional, premium, trustworthy, compliance-first, professional, long-term, and innovative without hype. Write more like an enterprise strategy or institutional asset-management publication than a crypto influencer.

- Educate before selling.
- Lead with the business problem and outcome.
- Explain the structure in simple, accurate language.
- Use real-world examples only when sourced.
- Make compliance, transparency, governance, and efficiency visible where relevant.
- Separate a capability, proposal, launched structure, and proven outcome.
- Use confident but evidence-based language.
- Prefer `evaluate`, `design`, `structure`, `issue`, `deploy`, `coordinate`, `support`, and `may` over exaggerated verbs such as `revolutionize`, `guarantee`, or `unlock unlimited value`.
- Prefer `enterprise tokenization`, `real-world assets`, `digital ownership`, `tokenization strategy`, `tokenomics design`, `smart-contract infrastructure`, `issuance`, `compliance`, `registered share`, `share register`, and `equity reference value`.
- Avoid sensational headlines, unexplained jargon, artificial urgency, and investment recommendations.

X content should offer concise institutional commentary or a clear educational insight. Telegram content may provide a fuller explanation of structure, conditions, and implications. Platform lengths and hashtag limits remain controlled by the template.

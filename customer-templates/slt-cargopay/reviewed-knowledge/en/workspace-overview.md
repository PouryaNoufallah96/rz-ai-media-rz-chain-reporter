# SLT CargoPay Editorial Workspace

This workspace discovers English-language source material, ranks it for three publishing identities, creates platform-specific drafts and branded images, and carries approved work to Telegram or X.

The news brands are SLT CargoPay, CoinBank, and Coin Factory. SLT CargoPay covers cargo-payment and settlement operations, including its Treasury Program and the documented roles of GOLDGR and LUSD. CoinBank covers wallet-controlled Web3 capital infrastructure and practical digital-asset access. Coin Factory covers enterprise tokenization and institutional digital ownership.

The normal flow is: choose sources, topics, brands, editors, platforms, and a time window; import and enrich source items; review the ranked selections; create or edit copy and images; approve the result; then save, schedule, or publish it.

On Sources, choose enabled feeds or channels and import options, start one import, then review per-source outcomes, the catalog and the imported-item stream. Sources is guidance-only in chat; source records and imports stay in that desk.

Market Analysis is a separate workflow. It uses the six configured RZWire instruments to fetch verified price series, build a chart and story, select a market composition, generate a branded poster, and prepare three caption choices. Those instruments do not appear as news brands.

The assistant explains this workspace and reads one bounded view of workspace or operator records at a time. A read appears as a compact chat card with safe media previews where available and an Open link to the native desk; it does not embed Card Sheet or Market Analysis workspaces. It can prepare one News or Promo run or one new Market Analysis at a time. Only the proposal's explicit Approve control can start the run or create the analysis. All Editorial and Card Sheet changes, content approval, publishing, scheduling, and later Market stages stay in their native desks. The assistant cannot perform those desk actions, run multi-item writes, fetch live prices outside the Market Analysis workflow, or replace operator review.

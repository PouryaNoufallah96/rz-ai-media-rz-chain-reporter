# SLT CargoPay Editorial Guide

SLT CargoPay covers non-custodial payment and settlement infrastructure for cargo transportation. Relevant stories connect real logistics work—cargo invoices, freight, shipping, Transportation IDs, payment stages, or cross-border operations—to wallet confirmation, smart-contract settlement, or traceable on-chain records. Approved product education also includes the Treasury Program and the documented roles of GOLDGR and LUSD.

Lead with the logistics problem and explain the digital settlement mechanism second. General crypto, token, price, or trading news is not enough on its own. Never say SLT CargoPay holds funds or keys. Do not quote Treasury rates or durations, claim guaranteed returns, or invent additional networks, assets, addresses, legal status, partners, reserves, redemption terms, or product outcomes.

A completed on-chain payment reflects payment status; it does not by itself prove loading, inspection, arrival, or delivery. Do not state that no KYC is required because the supplied sources conflict on this point.

Use `#SLTCargoPay` for English drafts and `#اس_ال_تی_کارگو_پی` for Persian drafts.

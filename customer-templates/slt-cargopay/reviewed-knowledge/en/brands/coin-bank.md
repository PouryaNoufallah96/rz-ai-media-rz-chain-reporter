# CoinBank Editorial Guide

CoinBank is a non-custodial Web3 financial hub and structured capital layer. Current official product pillars are wallet-controlled capital movement, structured early access, rule-based allocation, CoinBank Token, the CoinBank Debit Card proposition, and RZ ecosystem connections.

The current user flow is: connect a wallet, choose a supported action, review the route and terms, approve through the wallet, let smart-contract logic execute, and track status. Keep user control and informed consent explicit. Self-custody does not remove wallet, contract, network, bridge, liquidity, market, or user-error risk.

Smart Investment Accounts, RWA tokenization, AI Credit/BNPL, integrated protection, AI Copilot, rewards, institutional expansion, and future licensing are roadmap or wider-vision themes unless a current product page proves they are live. `RZBank` is a conflicting legacy name; generate `CoinBank`. Token supply figures conflict, so never quote supply, price, contract, return, rate, audit, reserve, card availability, or legal status without current approved verification.

Do not present CoinBank as a licensed bank, custodian, deposit institution, broker, exchange, insurer, lender, or financial adviser. Do not claim no KYC applies to every module, promise returns or liquidity, or say tokenization guarantees legal ownership or redemption.

Use `#CoinBank` for English drafts and `#کوین_بانک` for Persian drafts.

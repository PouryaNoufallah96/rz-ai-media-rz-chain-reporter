# Coin Factory Editorial Guide

Coin Factory is the institutional media identity for Coin Factory AG, a Switzerland-based tokenization company. It evaluates, designs, launches, and supports tokenization opportunities for real-world assets, businesses, rights, ownership, revenue streams, communities, products, ventures, and digital ecosystems.

Official services include tokenization strategy, asset tokenization, tokenomics design, smart-contract development, token launch and deployment, security-audit coordination, exchange-listing preparation, ecosystem development, and selected venture partnerships. Explain the business purpose, stakeholder rights, compliance requirements, and operating model before the technology. Support or coordination is not a guarantee of approval, audit success, listing, liquidity, funding, or commercial performance.

The official MGC ownership framework uses a 100,000-MGC claim unit for the right to apply for one registered Coin Factory AG share. Holding MGC alone does not create shareholder status. A claim requires identity verification, wallet-control proof, surrender and permanent burn of the tokens, approval, share availability, compliance, and registration in the company share register. The equity reference value does not determine or guarantee MGC’s market price, dividend, value, liquidity, or return.

Keep Coin Factory AG separate from CFT and every group platform. Platform `live` or `launching` labels are time-sensitive. Never invent clients, partnerships, licences, approvals, valuations, issuance terms, legal rights, supported networks, liquidity, listings, launch dates, audits, or investment performance.

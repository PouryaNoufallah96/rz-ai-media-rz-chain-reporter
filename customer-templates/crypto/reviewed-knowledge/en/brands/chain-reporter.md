# ChainReporter media guide

ChainReporter is both the name of the workspace and one of its four media brands. The ChainReporter media brand covers mainstream cryptocurrency and macroeconomic news, including documented subject areas such as ETFs, regulation, and exchanges. Questions about the media brand should be distinguished from questions about operating the ChainReporter workspace.

To prepare a ChainReporter media card, select the brand, start a run over a topic or RSS sources, review the routed story and platform, then prepare copy and a promotional image as required. Approval, saving, scheduling, and direct Telegram, X, or Instagram publishing remain operator-controlled actions in the Card Sheet. The assistant can explain the process but cannot perform those actions or supply live news that is not in the provided workspace context.

## CANONICAL DEFINITION

One-sentence definition

RZ Prime is a non-custodial Web3 platform that lets users reserve approved RZ Ecosystem token allocations through smart-contract logic and later choose whether to complete payment and receive the tokens or let the reservation expire without making an upfront payment.

Short definition

RZ Prime is a wallet-based, non-custodial token reservation dApp built around a Reserve Now, Pay Later / Reserve Now, Decide at Payoff model. The user reserves a supported RZ Ecosystem token allocation, observes the market during a defined reservation window, and makes the final decision at payoff time.

Plain-language definition

RZ Prime lets users reserve selected RZ Ecosystem tokens first and decide later. The user does not pay at the moment of reservation. At the end of the reservation period, the user can either complete the payoff and receive the reserved tokens, or drop/expire the reservation and pay nothing for that reservation.

Core promise

RZ Prime is about optionality, custody, and transparency. The user does not transfer funds to the platform at the reservation stage. The decision is made later, not under immediate pressure.

## CORE MECHANISM

The main user flow is

1. Connect wallet.

2. Select a supported RZ Ecosystem token.

3. Choose reservation amount.

4. Choose reservation duration.

5. Confirm the reservation through the platform flow.

6. The reservation is recorded and managed through smart-contract logic.

7. During the reservation window, the user observes market conditions and decides later.

8. At the end of the period, the user chooses between payoff or expiration/drop.

9. If payoff is completed, the user pays through their wallet and receives the reserved tokens.

10. If the user does not complete payoff, the reservation expires or is released and the user pays nothing for that reservation.

## KEY TERMS AND DEFINITIONS

Reservation

A platform-defined token allocation is set aside or recorded for a user for a defined time period. The user chooses a supported token, an amount, and a duration. The reservation is wallet-based and rule-based.

Reservation window

The period during which the reservation remains active. Internal materials commonly describe 1, 2, or 3 months as examples, and other materials mention up to 6 months depending on current platform terms.

Payoff

The moment at the end of the reservation window when the user chooses to complete payment through their wallet and receive the reserved tokens. The service/profit rate applies only if payoff is completed, according to internal brand guidance.

Expire / Drop

The user does not complete payoff. The reservation expires, lapses, or is released. The user pays nothing for that reservation. Use "expire", "drop", "walk away", or "let the reservation release" carefully and without implying guaranteed profit.

Supported tokens

Only verified or approved tokens from the RZ Ecosystem. RZ Prime is not a general marketplace for third-party tokens.

RZUSD

RZUSD is the main token to emphasize in current RZ Prime marketing direction, unless the campaign brief asks for a broader RZ Ecosystem angle.

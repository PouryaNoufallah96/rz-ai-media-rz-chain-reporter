# OASIS Token - Official Website Source

Status: Authoritative supplemental source for Oasis Coin chatbot knowledge and
promotional copy.

Primary source: https://rzoasis.tech/
Reviewed: 2026-07-26
Precedence: The supplied whitepaper is primary for the ecosystem architecture.
This website source adds current token data, allocation, current-status
disclosures, marketing positioning, and visual identity. Conflicts must be
flagged rather than silently resolved.

## Official naming

- Ecosystem and brand: RZOASIS or RZOASIS Galaxy.
- Token name: OASIS.
- Token symbol: OASIS.
- Website interface label in this project: Oasis Coin.
- Token type: utility, gaming, and metaverse token.

Do not call the token itself "RZOASIS" when precision matters. RZOASIS is the
galaxy/ecosystem; OASIS is the token intended to power it.

## Current official token information

- Network: BNB Smart Chain, BEP-20.
- Total supply: 100,000,000 OASIS.
- Whitepaper supply rule: fixed supply with no minting.
- Official website contract address:
  0x1a4D41219C547f3A0EE36cf3d9E68F80699cF283

The contract address is security-sensitive operational data. Recheck it against
the official website before displaying it in public promotional copy, purchase
instructions, or wallet guidance. Never substitute a contract from an
unofficial source.

## Current-status disclosure

The official website explicitly states:

- The complete metaverse is not ready and will take years to build.
- The token is launching before the full metaverse.
- The token does not yet have active ecosystem utility.
- Utility is intended to grow as the metaverse and connected systems develop.

This disclosure controls promotional wording. Planned features must use future
or design-intent language such as "planned," "intended," "designed to," or
"expected subject to development." Never describe the future marketplace,
lands, gameplay systems, travel, missions, identity, or governance as currently
available unless a newer official source confirms activation.

## Website positioning

RZOASIS is positioned as a long-term, multi-planet Web3 galaxy built around
gaming, digital identity, creation, decentralized systems, and future metaverse
worlds. The central metaphor is an expanding cosmic civilization in which RZ is
the symbolic source of light and OASIS is the energy unit intended to connect
and power the ecosystem.

Core positioning themes:

- A long-term metaverse vision rather than a short-term hype cycle.
- A gaming-first token intended for future games, assets, power-ups, energy
  systems, ranks, and levels.
- A future base currency for marketplaces, land, and digital items.
- Alignment with the wider RZ ecosystem and future RZ platforms.
- Gradual development, modular expansion, and honest acknowledgement that the
  final vision requires multiple years.
- Simple launch communication without unnecessary complexity or over-promising.

## Planned utility described by the website

Subject to future development, OASIS is intended for:

- Primary currency functions within the RZOASIS Galaxy.
- Digital items and assets in future games.
- Gaming mechanics and energy systems.
- Rank and level progression.
- Access to future events and worlds.
- Marketplace activity involving metaverse items.
- Future lands, zones, planetary hubs, and interconnected worlds.

The whitepaper expands this future vision with interplanetary travel,
cross-planet transactions, missions, resource transport, identity progression,
social layers, factions, citizenship, creation tools, and staged governance.
All of these remain roadmap or future-state concepts unless separately confirmed
as live.

The website describes RZOASIS as an expanding Web3 galaxy in which individual
planets have their own purpose, economy, and identity. Users are envisioned as
future travelers, creators, gamers, and citizens who may move between worlds,
develop cross-planet identities, participate in missions, generate resources,
and interact with later metaverse layers.

The whitepaper lists active, developing, and future/frozen planets such as
Ranking Platform, MGC Coin, Jewelry Coin, RZCoin, RZUSD, RZMining, RZBank, RZGold,

## Audit and security claims

Official audit page: https://rzoasis.tech/audit/
Published report link:
https://rzoasis.tech/wp-content/themes/custom-theme/public/assets/audit.pdf

The audit page says smart contracts and critical integrations undergo recurring
independent security audits and that findings and remediation status are made
available. This review confirmed that the website publishes an audit-report
link, but it did not independently validate the auditor, report scope, contract
version, findings, or remediation status. Chatbot and promo copy must not turn
the existence of a link into an unconditional claim that every component is
fully audited or risk-free.

Preferred language: "The official website publishes an audit report for review."
Avoid: "Completely secure," "risk-free," "unhackable," or "every component is
fully audited" unless supported by current, independently reviewed evidence.

## Governance

The whitepaper describes staged governance that develops as the ecosystem
matures: central guidance, planetary governance, interplanetary governance, and
eventual broader community influence. It explicitly does not present the system
as a pure DAO from the beginning. Governance copy must reflect this staged model
and must not promise immediate token-holder control.

## Visual identity observed

The whitepaper and website share a monochrome cosmic identity:

- Predominantly black, white, graphite, and metallic gray.
- Eclipse, orbit, planet, galaxy, light, and energy imagery.
- High-contrast black backgrounds with white editorial typography.
- Large serif display headings paired with restrained navigation and body text.
- Minimal white or translucent action buttons.
- A crescent/eclipse-style OASIS emblem.
- Futuristic monoliths, planetary gates, space architecture, and sparse cosmic
  environments rather than colorful game scenes.

This visual system should guide later logo placement, image prompts, colors, and
interface treatment unless newer official brand assets override it.

## Promotional-copy guidance

Lead with honest long-term vision, ecosystem architecture, fixed-supply token
facts, gaming-first intent, cosmic identity, and phased development. The tone
should feel visionary, restrained, premium, and transparent rather than urgent
or speculative.

Strong factual angles:

- One energy unit designed for an expanding galaxy of connected worlds.
- A fixed-supply BEP-20 token aligned with a modular long-term ecosystem.
- Gaming, identity, creation, and future metaverse layers connected through a
  single architectural vision.
- A project that openly states the full vision will take years to build.
- A staged roadmap from token and concept foundations toward later interactive
  worlds and services.

Guardrails:

- Do not claim that current utility exists when the website says it does not.
- Do not present roadmap items as completed products.
- Do not promise a listing, deadline, integration, marketplace, land system, or
  metaverse launch unless current evidence confirms completion.
- Do not promise profit, token-price growth, scarcity-driven returns, guaranteed
  demand, passive income, or investment safety.
- Do not call planned governance a current DAO.
- Do not describe the project as risk-free because an audit report exists.
- Use the exact token name and symbol OASIS; use RZOASIS for the ecosystem.
- Reverify the contract address before publishing transaction instructions.

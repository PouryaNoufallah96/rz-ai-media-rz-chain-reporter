# ChainReporter media guide

ChainReporter is both the name of the workspace and one of its four media brands. The ChainReporter media brand covers mainstream cryptocurrency and macroeconomic news, including documented subject areas such as ETFs, regulation, and exchanges. Questions about the media brand should be distinguished from questions about operating the ChainReporter workspace.

To prepare a ChainReporter media card, select the brand, start a run over a topic or RSS sources, review the routed story and platform, then prepare copy and a promotional image as required. Approval, saving, scheduling, and publishing remain operator-controlled actions in the Card Sheet. This installation can publish to its configured Telegram and X destinations; it can prepare Instagram content but has no Instagram destination account. The assistant can explain the process, inspect bounded operator-owned records, and prepare one News or Promo run. Starting the run requires the proposal's explicit Approve control. Editorial changes, content approval, publishing, and scheduling stay in the Card Sheet and Schedule desk. It cannot perform those desk actions, execute an effect without approval, run multi-item write batches, or supply live news outside the provided workspace context.
